About Dave

A voice built on
experience.

Long before voiceover became a professional pursuit, people had already recognized the depth, warmth and presence of Dave Fowler’s voice.

When Dave was in fourth grade, his voice began changing unusually early. His music teacher immediately noticed and told him, “You are going to have a strong, deep voice.”

That prediction proved accurate. Since around the age of eighteen, people have regularly stopped Dave—sometimes almost daily—to ask whether he works in radio. When he answered no, the response was nearly always the same: “Well, you should.”

“David Fowler, the Golden Voice of Victory.”

At his graduation from Victory Christian School in Tulsa, Oklahoma, that introduction gave a name to what others had already recognized: a voice with natural strength, warmth and presence.

Why Voiceover. Why Now.

A natural next chapter.

Voiceover brings together the disciplines Dave has spent his life developing: communication, storytelling, broadcasting, ministry, business, leadership and emotional connection.

This is not a sudden discovery of his voice. It is the decision to finally give that lifelong gift a focused professional outlet.

Clients are not simply hiring a sound. They are trusting someone to represent their company, mission, product or story. Dave approaches that responsibility with preparation, professionalism and genuine respect for the message behind every word.
